VERSION: 1.7.5
DETAILS:

new: Launcher at Pointer - Press your own shortcut and a compact Launcher opens right under your pointer, on whichever display you're on. Search, click an app and it closes; clicking anywhere else, pressing Esc or switching apps closes it too. It works with every bar layout and leaves your bar as it is. Turn it on in Settings → Hotkeys → Launcher at Pointer and record a shortcut!
new: Names on Bar - Show each item's name below, above, left or right of its Floating Bar icon, so you never have to guess what an icon or folder is. Find it in Settings → Appearance → Icon Labels → Names on Bar. Long names end in “…”, and hovering shows the full name. Thanks to Stefan for the idea!
new: DockFlow Widget: Move Dock to Locked Display - When DockFlow's Lock Dock is on and your Dock wanders to another display, one click in the DockFlow widget puts it back. If Lock Dock is off, the widget offers to open DockFlow's Lock Dock settings. Needs DockFlow 1.89 or later!
new: Export Logs - Settings → Diagnostics → Export Logs… saves one file you can send to support@appitstudio.com when something goes wrong. Your user name, email, file paths and web addresses are removed, and nothing is ever sent automatically!
improved: No More Freezes When the Bar Changes Size - Switching presets, adding or removing items, and changing alignment, bar size or placement now snap the Floating Bar into place at once instead of freezing the app for a moment!
improved: Menu Bar Mode Explained - With Menu Bar selected, Settings → Position now explains the ExtraBar icon, what the Show / Hide shortcut does, and why some menu bar icons can go missing on a full menu bar or under the notch!
improved: The Launcher Closes When You Click Another App - Even with “Background behind Launcher” off, it no longer stays open behind the app you clicked!
improved: AI Assistants No Longer Reopen ExtraBar - If you quit ExtraBar, a request from Claude or another AI assistant won't launch it again. You get a clear “open ExtraBar” message instead!
bug fix: Settings Titles No Longer Hide Under the Title Bar - Every Settings pane's title is visible at any window height, the sidebar scrolls, and new Settings windows open taller!
bug fix: Broken Action Links No Longer Block the Screen - An outdated or broken ExtraBar action link now shows a short notification explaining why it didn't work, instead of an alert that stopped everything!
bug fix: Keyboard Menus on Side Bars - With the Floating Bar on the left or right edge, menus opened from the keyboard now appear at the right icon!
