VERSION: 1.4.1
DETAILS:

new: Vertical bar positions — dock the floating bar to the left or right edge as a vertical bar, with five placements in all (Top, Left, Right, Top Left, Top Right) chosen via a visual screen-edge picker in Settings → Display Modes
new: Tint any bar item's icon into a single-color silhouette (White, Black, or an accent color) via right-click → Tint Icon; icons with a real shape only, full-color app icons untouched, and the choice travels with presets on export/import
new: Recycle Bin for Shiori bookmarks — deleting a bookmark moves it to a bin you can restore from, Delete Forever, Empty Trash, or search; deletions sync across devices and a restore always wins over a stale deletion
improved: Right-clicking (or ⌃-clicking) the collapse/menu-mode icon now opens the standard app menu in both floating-bar and menu-bar modes
improved: The default preset can now be deleted as long as it isn't active and isn't your last one — another preset is promoted to default automatically
improved: The bookmark Import sheet scrolls to show the result, and a zero-add import shows an orange "No Bookmarks Imported" instead of a misleading green "success"
improved: Bookmark delete confirmations now say the bookmark moves to the Recycle Bin and can be recovered; folder-only and mixed deletions still spell out what's permanent
